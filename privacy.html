Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

Dot It Down ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the Dot It Down mobile application on iOS and Android (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Data

Dot It Down is designed to be a private-first application. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect, transmit, or store personally identifiable information—such as your name, email address, or phone number—on any servers operated by us.

1.2 User-Generated Content

All tasks, schedules, completion history, and preferences you create within the App are stored locally on your device. We have no technical means of accessing your data or viewing your content in any form.

1.3 Local Storage and Backups

  • Local Database: Your tasks and settings are stored locally on your device in an on-device database and local preferences storage.
  • Automatic Cloud Backups: When Auto-backup is enabled (on by default), the App uses your device's native platform backup service—Apple iCloud on iOS and Google Drive / Android Auto Backup on Android—to automatically back up your task data and preferences to your personal cloud account so your data can be restored if you reinstall the App or switch devices. You can disable Auto-backup at any time in the App's Settings, which stops automatic cloud backups, excludes local data from system backups, and removes the App's cloud backup data.
  • Manual Export and Restore: You may manually export your tasks to a JSON file (Export data) or import a JSON backup file (Restore data) using your device's system file picker to save to local storage or a cloud service of your choice.

We do not operate external servers and do not host, transmit, or have access to these backups; all data remains strictly on your device and in your personal cloud or storage accounts under your control.

2. How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect your data, we do not use it for analytics, marketing, advertising, profiling, AI training, or any other purpose. The data you input is processed locally on your device solely to provide the App's core functionality (such as displaying your tasks, tracking completion streaks, and scheduling local reminders and notifications).

3. Permissions

The App may request or use the following device permissions to function correctly:

  • Notifications and Exact Alarms: To send local reminders for your tasks, optional daily morning and evening summaries, and the optional ongoing "Quick Add" notification on Android (including rescheduling active reminders after a device restart).
  • Biometrics and Device Authentication: To secure the App using Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint, or your device passcode when Lock app is enabled by you. Authentication is performed entirely by your device's operating system; the App never accesses or stores biometric data.
  • Files and Storage: To allow you to manually export and restore JSON backup files through your device's system file picker.
  • Timezone: To detect your device's local timezone so task reminders and daily summaries fire accurately in your current local time.

All permissions are optional and may be adjusted at any time in the App's Settings or your device's system settings.

4. Third-Party Services

  • App Stores and Platform Cloud Services: The App is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store, and relies on Apple iCloud (on iOS) and Google Play Services / Android Auto Backup (on Android) when automatic backups are enabled. These platform services are governed by Apple's and Google's respective privacy policies.
  • In-App Purchases: Payments for the one-time lifetime unlock (and purchase restorations) are processed securely through Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit) on iOS and Google Play Billing on Android. We do not receive, process, or store your credit card, billing, or payment details.
  • Diagnostics: We do not include third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs in the App. However, Apple and Google may collect basic installation or crash diagnostics in accordance with their own privacy policies and your device settings.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not store or retain any user data on external servers. Your data remains on your device—and, if Auto-backup is enabled, in your personal Apple iCloud or Google account—until you delete it. You can permanently delete all tasks and remove your cloud backup at any time using the Delete data option in the App's Settings, or disable Auto-backup to remove cloud backup data. Any manual JSON backup files you export remain in the location you saved them until you delete them.

6. Security

We prioritize the security of your data. Optional biometric or device-credential locking (Lock app) allows you to protect your local data from unauthorized access on your device, and platform cloud backups are protected by the security and encryption controls of your Apple iCloud or Google account. However, because your data is stored locally and in your personal accounts, security ultimately depends on the measures you apply to protect your device, cloud accounts, and exported backups.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from any user, including children. Because no data is collected, the App complies with applicable children's privacy regulations such as COPPA.

8. Legal Compliance

Because we do not collect, process, or transmit personal information, we are exempt from several provisions of data protection laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Nevertheless, we uphold privacy-first principles and respect all user rights under these frameworks.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically for updates.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our practices, please email us at help@dotitdown.app.
