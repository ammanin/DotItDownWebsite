DiD Founder

A note from the 1st user.

Dot It Down was created in December 2025 as a pet project to solve a personal need. I searched extensively for a task/habits app that was simple and fit the way my brain worked. I couldn’t find one that stuck, so I made one.

As I built and used DiD, I realized what I actually needed: a place to quickly drop random chores that popped into my head, and flexibly track non-regular, repeating things like washing my hair, watering plants, and so on. I didn’t want to feel guilty when I fell behind. I wanted a tool, not a system. Something that flexed to my life, not the other way around.

I also wanted to build differently. I’ve become increasingly disenchanted with big tech and wanted to try a new model: one driven primarily by usefulness, not investment returns.

Dot It Down is now available on Android and iPhone! Use it at your own pace and over time, I hope DiD lightens your mental load 💜

✌🏽 Ammani
