DiD Founder

A note from the 1st user.

I started building Dot It Down in December 2025 because I was tired of carrying a hundred little things in my head. Every app I tried just didn't work with my brain and turned into a new project to manage. So. I made my own.

As I built and used DiD, I realized what I needed was a place to quickly drop random chores that popped into my head, and flexibly track non-regular, repeating things like washing my hair, watering plants, or changing the sheets. I didn’t want to feel guilty when I fell behind. I wanted a tool, not a system. Something that flexed to my life, not the other way around.

I also wanted to build differently. I’ve become increasingly disenchanted with big tech and wanted to try a new model: one driven primarily by usefulness, not investment returns.

Dot It Down is now available on Android and iPhone! Use it at your own pace. Over time, I hope DiD lightens your mental load 💜

✌🏽 Ammani
