Hi — I'm the whole team. One dev, one designer, one QA, one everything.

I built Dot It Down because every other to-do app kept trying to become a productivity lifestyle. Streaks. Feeds. "You missed 3 days!" I just wanted somewhere to put a thought before it fell out of my head.

So: one-time price, no data leaves your phone, no account, no dashboards for me to stare at. Built quietly with a lot of coffee and a little AI. Zero crashes in seven months. Not trying to scale — trying to be useful.

If it helps you carry the day a little lighter, I'll consider it a win.
